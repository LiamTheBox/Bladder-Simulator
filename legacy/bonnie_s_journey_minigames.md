# Bonnie's Journey: Minigames Detail

Throughout the interactive journey, Bonnie's struggle to maintain composure is represented by several real-time minigames. Failing these minigames generally results in a drop in Control or a spike in Suspicion.

## 1. On The Spot (`spot`)
*   **UI Title:** ⚠️ ON THE SPOT
*   **Context:** Occurs during intense, unavoidable moments of physical pressure where Bonnie has no good options (e.g., enduring the painful lacing of her suit).
*   **Mechanic:** A live drain game. Bonnie's "Control" meter steadily drops in real-time on the screen. The player must quickly choose between two difficult options (e.g., "Ask for mercy" vs. "Endure it").
*   **Consequence:** The longer the player takes to decide, the more Control is permanently lost. Choosing to endure usually costs a heavy chunk of Control, while asking for mercy costs Suspicion and "Good Girl" points.

## 2. Mash (`mash`)
*   **UI Title:** ✋ PUT. THE CUP. DOWN. / 🛬 RIDE THE DESCENT
*   **Context:** Used for moments of sheer physical panic or when Bonnie's autopilot "people-pleasing" instincts try to take over. 
    *   *Cup Variant:* Triggers if Bonnie's "Good Girl" halo is too high and she starts automatically accepting a drink she can't afford to take.
    *   *Descent Variant:* Occurs during the agonizing 40-minute plane descent.
*   **Mechanic:** A frantic button-mashing game. The player must rapidly click a single button ("FORCE IT DOWN" or "HOLD THE LINE") to fill a progress bar before a timer runs out. The bar constantly drains backward, fighting the player's clicks.
*   **Consequence:** Winning saves Bonnie from a disastrous choice or grants a Control boost. Losing results in severe Control damage or being forced to drink another heavy beverage.

## 3. Rhythm (`rhythm`)
*   **UI Title:** 👣 SHUFFLE — CLAMP
*   **Context:** Encountered during the agonizingly slow 40-minute wait in the airport security queue.
*   **Mechanic:** A timing-based accuracy game. A white dot moves continuously back and forth across a horizontal bar. The player must click the "STEP" button exactly when the dot is inside the designated green "timing zone."
*   **Consequence:** Landing successful steps fills the requirement (e.g., 4 to 10 steps depending on intensity) and actually *restores* Control by using the rhythm as a distraction. Missing the timing zone counts as a "wobble," penalizing Control and raising Suspicion. Three misses result in a failure.

## 4. Hold (`hold`)
*   **UI Title:** 🗿 PERFECT STILLNESS
*   **Context:** Triggered when absolute, statue-like stillness is required, such as stepping into the TSA security scanner or enduring Mrs. Higgins's ferocious double-hug at the airport.
*   **Mechanic:** Press and hold. The player must click and hold down a button ("HOLD STILL") for a set duration to fill a meter. Crucially, random, frantic text prompts will flash on screen (e.g., *"a SPASM hits — hold..."* or *"an itch, a twitch — HOLD..."*) designed to spook the player into accidentally letting go.
*   **Consequence:** Releasing the button early counts as a flinch. This heavily damages Control and raises Suspicion, as nearby characters notice her sudden, jerky movements.

## 5. Volley (`volley`)
*   **UI Title:** 💬 KEEP UP THE CHATTER
*   **Context:** Rapid-fire social interactions where Bonnie must mask her desperation with bright conversation (used with Chloe, the Immigration Officer, Irina, and Mrs. Higgins).
*   **Mechanic:** A timed dialogue choice game. A question appears on screen, and the player is presented with two possible answers. A timer bar drains extremely quickly. The player must click an answer before the bar empties.
*   **Consequence:** Hesitating (letting the timer run out) results in an awkward pause that spikes Suspicion. Answering quickly and confidently clears the volley, lowering Suspicion and rewarding the player for their flawless social mask.

## 6. Taps (`taps`)
*   **UI Title:** ✈️ BRACE FOR TURBULENCE
*   **Context:** Occurs during the 5th hour of the flight when the plane hits a rough patch of turbulence, bouncing the cabin around.
*   **Mechanic:** A reaction-speed matching game. An arrow flashes on screen (Left ⬅, Right ➡, or Both ⬆). The player has a fraction of a second to click the corresponding button before the "jolt" lands.
*   **Consequence:** Successfully bracing protects Bonnie's Control. Failing to match the arrow in time means the jolt lands directly on her strained bladder, causing immediate and severe Control damage. Two misses result in a total failure for that event.
