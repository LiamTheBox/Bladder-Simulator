    *   Don't flush. Ease the lid down, leave silently, flush 'casually' after everyone wakes.
    *   Flush and sprint. The rattle races up the pipes as I race up the stairs.

*(Note: If Bonnie makes noise, a "Stir" event occurs with the following choices:)*
